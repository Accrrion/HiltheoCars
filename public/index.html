Our Heritage

An Igbo kitchen,
in upscale Lekki.

Ofe Uto means "sweet soup" in Igbo — and sweetness is exactly what we serve. Nestled on the 3rd Floor of Westbrook Mall in the heart of Lekki Phase 1, we are Lagos's most authentic window into Eastern Nigerian cuisine.

Every detail of Ofe Uto is intentional. Our waiters dress in traditional Igbo attire — george and isi-agu. The decor — hand-selected fabrics, earthy tones, uli patterns and ancestral masks — transports you to the lush forests and vibrant markets of the East. We don't just cook food; we preserve a legacy.

Our recipes are rooted in generations of Igbo culinary wisdom, passed from grandmothers to mothers, from village kitchens to the city, arriving now at your table — simmered slow, seasoned deep, and served with pride.
